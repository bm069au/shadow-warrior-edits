This is my Shadow Warrior 1997 Mod Fork. 
There are two complete exe files here and for Windows 10/11. You are free to download anything including the code but if you do and build something else cool, I expect you to share with me so I can share the laughs. 

What this does: 
1. Nuclear Grenades (Grenades are now nuclear weapons)
2. Caltrops set you on fire with fire damage
3. Shurikans set you on fire with fire damage
4. Brand new weapon.. Staler mines, a subset of the Stickybomb.
5. Rare drops. 1. Guardian head on nuke drop, and 2. The 'rifle' on death. Both are set to 5% chance same as the Boss bunny. 

6. Bug fix: Exit even if multiplayer host quits first. No more Windows PC Hangs.
7. Stickybombs will not drop in the last 60 seconds of multiplayer game to prevent timer issues on level finish.

8. Note: Boss bunny grows larger, more deadly and harder to kill for the first 4 nukes but the 5th nuke kills it.
9. You can kill it with normal weapons but its a Boss so really hard to kill. Also.. did I mention it fires fireballs at you and can bite you ?
10.  This is as far as I took it.. I play this with my friend Darrin about 3-4 times a week and we have been playing the game since 1997.
11.  If you decide to mod this further, do me a favor and drop me an email and share your results so I can have a laugh too.
