New Weapons, Controls & Mechanics Quick Reference

  • Key 4 — 3-Mode Grenade Launcher Toggle: Press 4 repeatedly while holding the Grenade Launcher to cycle between: (1) Normal 40mm Grenades, (2) Nuclear Grenades (Red tint, full Nuke blast), and (3) Ice Nuke Grenades (Cyan tint — freezes everyone in the blast radius for 10 seconds, strips armor to 0, drops HP to 20). Grenades also fire cleanly through roof holes and over outdoor walls/rooves!
  • Key 8 — Authentic Duke Nukem 3D Shrink Ray: Fires green SHRINKSPARK projectiles that reflect off mirrors! Shrunken players smoothly shrink over 1s, stay mouse-sized for 10s with Duke's pumping tiny fists on screen, can run into Mouse-Hole Tunnels, get squished if stepped on by a full-sized player, and get crushed if they unshrink inside a low tunnel.
  • Key 0 — Authentic Duke Nukem 3D Freezethrower: Fires bouncing icy blue FREEZEBLAST crystalline shots (bounces up to 3x off walls). Freezes victims solid blue for 10s (0 armor, 20 HP). Walk up and kick/shoot a frozen player to shatter them into 40 flying Duke 3D ice shards!
  • Key G — Grapple Hook: Fires an anchoring chain hook to pull yourself up into the high Grapple Sniper Lofts and Balconies.
  • Booby-Trap Wall Cracks (Red Squares on Map): Hidden rooms have linked cracks inside and outside on the main wall. Shoot or grenade the outside crack (or inside crack) to trigger a massive chain-reaction explosion inside the hiding room!
